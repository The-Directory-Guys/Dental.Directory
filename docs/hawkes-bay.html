Frequently asked questions

How much does a dental checkup cost in Hawke's Bay?

Checkup prices in Hawke's Bay vary by practice. Compare published checkup fees for individual clinics in the listings above, and ask what's included (some bundle X-rays or a clean) when you book.

Is there free or low-cost dental care in Hawke's Bay?

Dental care is free for children and teenagers up to their 18th birthday through the Community Dental Service and school dental clinics. Adults on a low income may qualify for a Work and Income (WINZ) dental grant of up to $1,000 a year for emergency treatment, and ACC covers dental treatment needed because of an accident or injury. Some practices also offer reduced fees for Community Services Card holders.

Are there emergency dentists in Hawke's Bay?

Yes. A number of Hawke's Bay practices offer same-day emergency appointments for issues like severe toothache, broken or knocked-out teeth and dental trauma. Use the Emergency service filter above to see clinics that provide urgent care, and call ahead to confirm availability.

Do Hawke's Bay dentists offer payment plans?

Many do. Practices across Hawke's Bay accept options such as Q Card, Afterpay, Zip and Gem Visa, and some offer interest-free plans for larger treatments like implants or orthodontics. Accepted payment options are listed on each clinic's profile.

How much is a hygienist visit in Hawke's Bay?

A hygienist visit is a professional clean. Prices in Hawke's Bay vary by practice and appointment length. Compare hygienist fees for individual clinics using the Max Hygienist Price filter above.

How do I choose the right dentist in Hawke's Bay?

Compare practices on what matters to you: patient ratings and review counts, published prices, location and the specific services you need (such as orthodontics, implants or teen dental). Dental Compare lets you filter by all of these and view up to four clinics side by side before you book.
