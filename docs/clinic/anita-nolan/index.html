Meet the team

DA
Dr. Anita Nolan
Oral medicine specialist. Diagnosis and medical management of disorders associated with the mouth, face, salivary glands and jaws. Specialist interests include oral lesions in Crohn's Disease, salivary gland disease and osteonecrosis of the jaw (ONJ). Care for patients with white and red patches, lumps and swellings, mouth ulcers, abnormal sensations of the mouth including burning mouth syndrome, facial pain including TMJ problems and trigeminal neuralgia, dry mouth and salivary gland disorders, oral complications of general disease, infectious diseases such as oral Candida and viral infections, oral cancer and pre-cancer, adverse side effects of drugs and other materials on the mouth.
