Frequently asked questions

How much does a dental checkup cost in Hamilton?

A standard dental checkup in Hamilton generally costs between $60 and $130, with most practices around $95. Many clinics bundle the exam with X-rays or a hygienist clean, so it's worth asking what's included when you book.

Is there free or low-cost dental care in Hamilton?

Dental care is free for children and teenagers up to their 18th birthday through the Community Dental Service and school dental clinics. Adults on a low income may qualify for a Work and Income (WINZ) dental grant of up to $1,000 a year for emergency treatment, and ACC covers dental treatment needed because of an accident or injury. Some practices also offer reduced fees for Community Services Card holders.

Are there emergency dentists in Hamilton?

Yes. A number of Hamilton practices offer same-day emergency appointments for issues like severe toothache, broken or knocked-out teeth and dental trauma. Use the Emergency service filter above to see clinics that provide urgent care, and call ahead to confirm availability.

Do Hamilton dentists offer payment plans?

Many do. Practices across Hamilton accept options such as Q Card, Afterpay, Zip and Gem Visa, and some offer interest-free plans for larger treatments like implants or orthodontics. Accepted payment options are listed on each clinic's profile.

How much is a hygienist visit in Hamilton?

A hygienist visit is a professional clean. Prices in Hamilton vary by practice and appointment length. Compare hygienist fees for individual clinics using the Max Hygienist Price filter above.

How do I choose the right dentist in Hamilton?

Compare practices on what matters to you: patient ratings and review counts, published prices, location and the specific services you need (such as orthodontics, implants or teen dental). Dental Compare lets you filter by all of these and view up to four clinics side by side before you book.
